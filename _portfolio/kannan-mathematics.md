# KANNAN Mathematics Series — Basic 2 Mathematics

## Educational Mathematics Textbook

A complete mathematics textbook production project developed for primary-level learners and aligned with the Ghanaian curriculum.

![Textbook Cover](/img/textbook-cover.jpg)

---

## Project Overview

This project involved the structured typesetting and visual production of a mathematics textbook of more than 200 pages.

The book covers:

- Number
- Algebra
- Geometry — Shapes and Space
- Measurement
- Data Handling

The objective was to create a structured, readable, visually consistent, and pupil-friendly educational publication.
